### 4.7 The Firewall Test

When in doubt, apply this three-question test:

1. **Can this statement be falsified by experiment?**

2. **Would removing all Track B references change the evidentiary status of this claim?**
   - Yes → Contamination detected. Remove Track B references.
   - No → References are contextual/aesthetic. Permissible with explicit separation.

3. **Could a reader reasonably interpret this as "the fiction proves the science" or "the science validates the fiction"?**
   - Yes → Rewrite with explicit separation.
   - No → Acceptable.

### 6.4 The α-invariance gate (Step 4)

published as a negative diagnostic.** The script must auto-invalidate.

**Symbol discipline:** in this contract the name α is reserved for the
time-acceleration factor. The 1/f noise exponent is cited as "noise exponent
≈ 1" to avoid a gauge-name collision.

**Extension beyond simulations (contract-level generalization, provisional):**
for empirical reconstruction pipelines (e.g. Module G on open datasets) and
analog readouts, the analogous threat is not solver step but reconstruction
discretization. The gate applies there as **refinement-invariance
("α-cousin")**: the verdict is recomputed under a pre-registered refinement
of the reconstruction (sampling rate, window length, τ and m within their
estimator-defined admissible ranges), with the tolerance pre-registered in

reserved for the simulation doctrine. This extension is proposed by a resonator leg and is provisional until Human Anchor acceptance is recorded

## 7. Observation & Telemetry Discipline

### 7.1 Three layers of a telemetry artifact

Every telemetry artifact separates, explicitly and in this order:

- **RAW** — numbers exactly as returned by the source API, with source id,
  fetch timestamp, and window definition. Measured.
- **DERIVED** — ratios, deltas, clone-per-unique factors, window-normalized
  rates. Computed; every formula stated next to the number it produces.
- **INTERPRETED** — causal or intentional reading ("pulse synchronized with
  commit", "automation signature"). Argument; must cite controls (§7.4) and
  the rules of this section; labeled as interpretation, never as measurement.

Only RAW and DERIVED may be quoted in Track A documents, and only as
observation. The INTERPRETED layer may enter Track A as a labeled hypothesis
about reach; it may never enter an evidentiary chain about science.

→ Source: TELEMETRY_2026-09-24.md; Phase 1 module folder schema (artifacts

  observations; overlapping windows must not be summed or compared as growth.
- Rate before count: comparisons use rates (clones/views, unique/clones,

  out-of-enum value is a **PARSE_FAIL**: the parser stops, reports the exact
  field, and re-fetches the document. Parsers never invent defaults. Fail
  loud, never silent.

  document with `contract.version` in the block. Mismatch, or an empty
  value, is PARSE_FAIL (internal inconsistency), fixed in one commit. The
  placeholder `v0.?` is a **valid draft value**: parsers accept it and must
  declare draft state in the first output (§9.5 step 5).

  never a numeric field inside the block. Older documents remain valid
